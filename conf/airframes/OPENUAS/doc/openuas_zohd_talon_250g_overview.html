Battery Discipline (2S Li-Ion)

  • Launch full: 8.4 V. Li-Ion sags more than LiPo — softer climb late in flight.
  • Plan landing at 6.6–6.4 V under load; hard floor 6.0 V.
  • Below 10 °C, warm the pack — cold Li-Ion sags hard on launch.

Hand Launch (motor ON)

  • Full throttle before release — the 5×5 pusher is behind you, clear of your arm.
  • Firm level throw, ≥ 9 m/s, nose 8–12° up, wings level.
  • Hands off is fine: the aircraft accelerates and settles into a ≈20° climb on its own.
  • Weak flat throws (≤ 8 m/s, < 6°) will mush and settle — commit to the toss.

Landing (belly)

  • Approach 8–9 m/s, power near idle; the clean airframe floats — plan a long shallow final.
  • Kill throttle over the threshold; flare to ≈10° and let it settle on the belly.
  • Prop is protected at the tail; grass or dirt strips preferred over gravel.

Handling Peculiarities

  • Pusher throttle coupling — power changes pitch trim noticeably; add throttle smoothly on approach.
  • No prop wash over tails — unlike a tractor, controls do NOT firm up with throttle at low airspeed; keep airspeed up during go-arounds.
  • Stall — gentle mush at 16.5° α with mild wing drop; recovery is immediate on relaxing elevator. Height loss ≈ 5–8 m.
  • Spiral mode — slowly divergent (≈20° bank per minute hands-off); normal for the class, the autopilot holds it easily.
  • Wind — at 37.9 g/dm² loading, stay below ≈8 m/s wind; penetration reserve is 16 m/s Vmax.
  • Adverse yaw — small; a touch of rudder mix (10–15 %) tidies up turn entries.
