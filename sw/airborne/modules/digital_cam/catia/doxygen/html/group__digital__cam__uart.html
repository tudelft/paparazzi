◆ digital_cam_uart_shoot()

uint8_t digital_cam_uart_shoot ( uint8_t camera_id,
bool report )
extern

Request a pose-tagged capture from one camera or all cameras.

Parameters
camera_idCATIA camera ID, or CATIA_CAMERA_ALL.
reportWhen true, emit DC_SHOT telemetry; when false, only advance numbering.
Returns
Zero when the complete CATIA frame was queued, nonzero otherwise.
Note
The photo number advances even when the serial frame cannot be queued, preserving monotonic numbering across best-effort high-rate capture requests.
