Detailed Description

Connected-component extraction of hot regions from Tiny1-C temperature planes.

Temperatures remain in the camera's packed 16-bit representation at this boundary. Detection converts only what it needs, groups adjacent threshold crossings, and reports geometry plus peak/mean Celsius values for later fire filtering or EXIF.
