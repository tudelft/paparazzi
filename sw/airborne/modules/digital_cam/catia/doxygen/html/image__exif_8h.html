Add flight metadata with optional capture-delay motion compensation.

Parameters
filenameExisting JPEG to rewrite atomically.
shotFlight-controller shot payload.
capture_delay_sNonnegative request-to-frame latency, or a negative value when unknown.
compensateNonzero to estimate ground displacement from speed/course and latency.

Original coordinates remain in the textual record so downstream analysis can audit the correction rather than treating the constant-velocity estimate as ground truth.
