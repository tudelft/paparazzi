Detailed Description

CATIA-side lifecycle, sample collection, and location fusion for EARcam.

A persistent EARcam child produces newline-delimited acoustic measurements. A reader thread retains only the latest measurement while CATIA geotags it at shots; the bounded session is then fused into a loudest-ground-spot estimate and can be rendered as an acoustic JPEG. Missing microphones remain nonfatal and are retried.
