Undistort a hotspot pixel, rotate its camera ray, and intersect it with ground.

Returns
False when calibration/pose is invalid or the ray is too close to the horizon.

The minimum ground cosine rejects ill-conditioned long-range intersections; callers must report such cases as unavailable rather than extrapolating a false location.
